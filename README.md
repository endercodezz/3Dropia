\# 3Dropia



A tiny Windows frontend for \*\*FBI Remote Install\*\* on Nintendo 3DS.



Drag in your files, enter your 3DS IP, and send them over your local network.



\## Features



\- Simple Windows GUI

\- Drag \& drop

\- `.cia`, `.tik`, `.cetk`, `.3dsx`

\- Multiple files

\- White / Black themes

\- Configurable FBI and HTTP ports

\- Remembers your settings



\## Usage



1\. Open \*\*FBI\*\* on your 3DS

2\. Go to `Remote Install → Receive URLs`

3\. Open \*\*3Dropia\*\*

4\. Add your files

5\. Press \*\*Send to 3DS\*\*
